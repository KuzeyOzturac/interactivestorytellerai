# Design

## Aesthetic direction

A late-night literary mystery with a warm amber accent and a story column opposite one full-bleed character illustration.

## Type and color

Literata for story prose and titles, DM Sans for controls and labels. Charcoal green background (#141715), pale parchment text (#ede6d8), and amber actions (#e4b579). Reading width stops at 65 characters.

## Layout and motion

The desktop reading room uses a 1.55:1 grid. Small screens stack the story and art. The journal and world editor use native modal dialogs. The interface uses no decorative animation.

## Components

Masthead, transcript, suggested actions, composer, branch controls, illustration frame, world editor, campaign journal. Art states distinguish a completed illustration, a prior reference, and an empty scene. Focus outlines use amber.
